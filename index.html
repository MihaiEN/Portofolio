E02 Live case study

Aspire Prep · Specialist Mathematics Tutoring

IB Mathematics · IGCSE Higher · Diagnostics · Live digital teaching

Turning a live tutoring session into a documented cycle of diagnosis, explanation, practice and feedback.

IB Mathematics IGCSE Higher ClassIn Shared whiteboard Custom assessment Feedback reports

At Aspire Prep I support high-attaining international learners in IB Mathematics and IGCSE Higher Mathematics. The tutoring model is deliberately evidence-led: I use live digital teaching and a shared interactive whiteboard to see the learner's reasoning as it develops, then use that evidence to design targeted practice, diagnostic assessments and written feedback for the next stage of teaching.

01DiagnoseEstablish what the learner can already do and where access breaks down
02Teach liveClassIn lesson with simultaneous tutor-student whiteboard working
03Capture reasoningAnnotated mathematics, method choices and misconceptions remain visible
04Target practiceCustom IB / IGCSE questions built around the observed gaps
05Report & adaptFeedback identifies strengths, priorities and the next teaching sequence

Live lesson environment

Two-way mathematics, not screen-sharing a worksheet.

ClassIn provides the lesson space while a simultaneous interactive whiteboard lets both tutor and student write on the same mathematical task. This makes working visible in real time: I can question a step, annotate an error, model an alternative method and ask the learner to continue from the exact point of difficulty rather than waiting for a final answer.

Specification-to-paper mapping

Coverage is designed before questions are finalised.

A paper is not treated as a collection of isolated questions. Each item needs a clear relationship to the intended learning outcome, the required command or skill and the overall balance of the examination.

IBM · Level 2
  • Business maths foundations
  • Data, graphs and interpretation
  • Ratios, currency and probability
  • Calculus and dispersion
FAB · Level 3
  • Costs, revenue and break-even
  • Cash-flow forecasting
  • Accounting statements
  • Financial and ratio analysis
01 · Coverage

Specification fidelity

Questions are checked against the published learning outcomes and assessment criteria so the examination samples the intended unit rather than drifting into adjacent content.

02 · Validity

Right task, right evidence

Command wording, context and mathematical or financial demand are reviewed so candidate responses provide evidence of the intended knowledge or skill.

03 · Balance

Calculation and explanation

Paper design considers the balance between computational work, interpretation and explanatory judgement instead of over-relying on one response type.

04 · Reliability

Marking consistency

Mark schemes, acceptable methods and standardisation decisions are written to reduce avoidable marker variation and support consistent outcomes.

Mark scheme design

A question is only useful if it can be marked reliably.

MethodRecognise valid mathematical / financial process
AccuracyCredit correct execution and final outcome
ExplanationDefine what sufficient written evidence looks like
Alternative routesAllow equivalent valid approaches where appropriate

This makes the mark scheme part of the assessment design process rather than an afterthought written once the paper is complete.

This work demonstrates assessment literacy across the full cycle: reading a specification, designing valid evidence opportunities, making those questions reliably markable, applying a consistent standard and using marking evidence to improve future assessment.

Responsible AI in education

AI is useful when the teacher remains accountable for the final decision.

I use generative AI selectively to accelerate early-stage drafting, create alternative examples, prototype assessment ideas, support resource adaptation and structure feedback. Mathematical correctness, specification alignment, tone and safeguarding are then checked manually before anything reaches a learner.

My professional development includes the Google Generative AI for Educators Certificate, and my approach is deliberately human-in-the-loop: AI can assist the workflow, but subject expertise and professional responsibility remain central.
