Professional approach

Four principles I want the portfolio to demonstrate.

01

Subject precision

Clear notation, carefully chosen examples and explanations that preserve the mathematics rather than oversimplify it.

02

Assessment with purpose

Questions should reveal reasoning and misconceptions, then feed directly into feedback and future teaching.

03

Evidence before claims

Professional statements are strongest when paired with a redacted artefact, outcome or reflection that shows the work.

04

Technology with judgement

Digital tools and AI should support professional reasoning, not replace subject expertise, safeguarding or teacher responsibility.
